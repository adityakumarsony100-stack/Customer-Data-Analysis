# 📊 Customer Behavior Analytics

An end-to-end **Data Analytics project** where I analyzed customer shopping behavior to uncover patterns in purchasing, customer segments, product categories, discounts, and sales channels.

I built this project to demonstrate how I would approach a real-world business analytics problem — starting with raw data and turning it into actionable insights.

## 🎯 Business Problem

The goal of this project was to answer:

> **How can a retail company use customer data to better understand purchasing behavior, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?**

I focused on questions such as:

* Which customer segments spend and purchase the most?
* Which product categories perform best?
* How do discounts affect purchasing behavior?
* What factors are associated with repeat purchases?
* How do online and offline customers differ?
* Which payment methods and customer groups are most common?

## 🔄 My Approach

I followed a complete end-to-end workflow:

**Raw Data → Data Cleaning → EDA → SQL Analysis → Power BI Dashboard → Insights & Recommendations**

### 🐍 Python

I cleaned and prepared the dataset, handled data quality issues, performed exploratory analysis, and identified important patterns in customer behavior.

### 🗄️ SQL

I used SQL to analyze customer segments, purchase behavior, product categories, discounts, sales channels, and customer loyalty.

### 📊 Power BI

I created an interactive dashboard to present key KPIs, customer insights, product performance, and purchasing trends in a business-friendly format.

### 💡 Business Insights

Finally, I translated the analysis into practical insights and recommendations related to customer retention, targeted marketing, promotions, and product strategy.
